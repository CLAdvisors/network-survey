Contemporary Leadership Advisors

Employee Survey Platform Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Contemporary Leadership Advisors (“CLA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides an employee survey and organizational analytics platform used to conduct employee engagement surveys, organizational health surveys, team assessments, organizational network analysis (“ONA”), and related assessments and research.

We recognize that employees are more likely to provide candid and useful feedback when they understand how their information will be used and protected. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the platform, why we collect it, how we protect it, and how survey results may be used and reported.

1. Confidentiality and Anonymity

Surveys conducted through the CLA platform are confidential, but not necessarily anonymous.

CLA may be able to associate a participant’s responses with their identity. Maintaining this association allows us to administer surveys effectively, track participation and send reminders, conduct organizational and network analyses, perform longitudinal and item-level analysis, conduct research, and improve our assessments and methodologies.

Although CLA may maintain identifiable survey data, we protect the confidentiality of individual responses as described in this Policy. Unless participants are expressly informed otherwise for a particular assessment, CLA does not disclose an identifiable participant’s individual survey responses to their employer.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the survey or assessment, CLA may collect and process:

  • Name, email address, employee identifier, or other information used to identify survey participants;
  • Survey responses, ratings, selections, and open-ended comments;
  • Information about whether an individual has started or completed a survey;
  • Organizational information, such as company, business unit, function, department, location, level, role, or tenure;
  • Demographic or other employee information supplied to CLA by the participant’s employer;
  • For ONA and similar assessments, information about professional relationships between individuals, including the identity of the person providing a response and the individuals identified in that response; and
  • Technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the platform.

CLA does not ask survey participants to provide special-category or sensitive demographic information through the survey platform. An employer may, however, provide demographic or other employee information to CLA for analytical purposes. When we receive such information, we protect and use it in accordance with this Policy and applicable law.

3. How We Use Information

CLA may use information collected through the platform to:

  • Administer surveys and assessments;
  • Manage participation and send survey reminders;
  • Analyze employee engagement, organizational health, team effectiveness, organizational networks, and related organizational characteristics;
  • Create reports, analyses, visualizations, and recommendations for our clients;
  • Compare results over time;
  • Conduct item-level and other statistical analysis;
  • Develop and validate survey instruments, assessments, analytical methodologies, and services;
  • Develop benchmarks using de-identified information;
  • Conduct research;
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the platform and our services; and
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

We use personal information only for purposes reasonably related to the survey, assessment, research, or services for which it was collected or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

4. How We Report Survey Results

Individual Survey Responses

Individual survey responses are confidential. Unless participants have been expressly informed otherwise for a particular assessment, CLA does not provide an employer with an identifiable individual’s responses to specific survey questions.

Aggregate Reporting

Engagement surveys, organizational health surveys, team assessments, and similar surveys are generally reported at the group or aggregate level. CLA generally requires a minimum of five survey respondents before reporting results for a particular group or subgroup. CLA may use a higher threshold or suppress particular results when we believe additional protection is appropriate to reduce the risk that individual responses could reasonably be identified.

Individually Identifiable Analytical Findings

Certain forms of organizational analysis, particularly ONA, inherently involve information about identifiable individuals. For example, an analysis may identify an individual as an important organizational connector, broker, influencer, or source of expertise. CLA may report an individually identifiable analytical finding when identifying the individual is relevant to the stated purpose of the assessment. When we do so, we do not disclose the individual’s underlying survey responses or identify which participants nominated or provided information about that individual. CLA will not report individually identifiable findings that are evaluative, critical, sensitive, or reasonably likely to disadvantage an individual unless participants have been specifically informed in advance that individual-level reporting of such findings is a purpose of the assessment.

5. Organizational Network Analysis

ONA differs from a traditional employee survey because the relationships between individuals are themselves the subject of analysis. For example, participants may be asked to identify colleagues they rely upon for information, advice, collaboration, decision-making, or other purposes. To conduct this analysis, CLA must be able to identify both the participant providing the response and the individuals identified by that participant. Accordingly, ONA responses are not anonymous.

CLA may use this information to analyze organizational networks and may report information about identifiable individuals as described in Section 4. However, CLA does not disclose to an employer how an identifiable participant answered a particular ONA question or which participant nominated a particular individual.

6. Open-Ended Responses

Open-ended survey responses are treated as confidential and are not attributed to individual respondents in reports provided to clients. Open-ended responses are subject to the same minimum reporting threshold of five respondents applicable to other survey results.

Participants should be aware, however, that the content, circumstances described, or writing style of an open-ended response may sometimes make its author recognizable. We therefore encourage participants not to include their name or unnecessary identifying information in an open-ended response if they wish to protect their confidentiality.

CLA may, at its discretion, redact, summarize, categorize, or withhold an open-ended response when we believe doing so is appropriate to protect confidentiality, personal privacy, or other legitimate interests.

7. Research and Benchmarking

CLA may use survey and assessment data to conduct research, develop organizational benchmarks, validate and improve our methodologies and assessments, and develop insights about employees, teams, leadership, and organizations. For these purposes, we may combine de-identified information from multiple client organizations.

Before information is included in benchmark datasets used on an ongoing basis or disclosed in published research or other external research materials, CLA takes steps to de-identify the information so that individual participants and client organizations cannot reasonably be identified. CLA may retain de-identified information indefinitely for research, benchmarking, methodology development, validation, trend analysis, and similar purposes.

8. Data Retention

By default, CLA retains identifiable survey and assessment data for up to three years following completion of the applicable survey or project. A different retention period may apply where specified in CLA’s agreement with the applicable client.

We retain identifiable information during this period to support activities such as client analysis, longitudinal comparisons, research, methodology development and validation, and administration of our services. At the end of the applicable retention period, identifiable survey data will generally be deleted or de-identified unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law or is reasonably necessary for another legitimate purpose.

Information that has been irreversibly de-identified may be retained indefinitely. Information may also remain temporarily in system backups until those backups are overwritten or deleted in accordance with our normal backup and data-management procedures.

9. Requests to Access, Correct, or Delete Information

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the survey, participants may have rights concerning personal information CLA maintains about them, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or other actions.

Participants may request deletion of personal information associated with them. CLA will honor verified deletion requests where required by applicable law and, where reasonably practicable, in other circumstances.

Certain information may be retained where necessary or permitted to comply with legal obligations, establish or defend legal claims, protect the rights of others, preserve the security or integrity of our systems or data, or satisfy other legitimate purposes permitted by applicable law. Once information has been irreversibly de-identified so that CLA can no longer reasonably associate it with an individual, it cannot subsequently be identified and deleted as that individual's personal information.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information in Section 15.

10. Service Providers

CLA uses third-party service providers to operate, host, maintain, secure, and support our survey platform and related services. We may provide personal information to these service providers only as reasonably necessary for them to perform services on our behalf. We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to protect that information through appropriate contractual confidentiality, privacy, and data-security obligations. CLA will make a list of the sub-processors that process personal information on our behalf available to clients upon request.

CLA does not sell survey participants’ personal information.

11. Data Security

CLA maintains reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or destruction. No information system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we therefore cannot guarantee the absolute security of information processed through the platform. In the event of a data-security incident affecting personal information, CLA will notify affected parties and clients as required by applicable law.

12. Legal Disclosures

CLA may disclose personal information when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, subpoena, court order, or governmental request. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of CLA, our clients, survey participants, our systems, or others, as permitted by applicable law.

13. International Data Protection

CLA's surveys may include participants located in the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, and other jurisdictions with specific privacy requirements.

Where applicable, CLA processes personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection laws, including requirements concerning transparency, data minimization, security, retention, individual rights, and lawful processing. Where personal information is transferred across national borders, CLA will use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

Depending on the particular engagement and applicable law, the participant's employer may determine the purposes for which employee information is collected and processed, while CLA processes information in connection with providing survey, assessment, analytics, and related services. The respective responsibilities of CLA and its clients may also be addressed in applicable agreements between CLA and the client.

Participants located in jurisdictions that provide additional privacy rights may contact CLA using the information below to exercise those rights.

14. Changes to This Policy

CLA may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, practices, technology, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date of this Policy and take other appropriate steps to provide notice where required by applicable law.
